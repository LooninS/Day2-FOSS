# Task 5: The Vanishing Flag

## The Story

Arjun, the maintainer of this repo, had found something valuable: a treasure he wanted only the sharpest minds to claim.

But he knew a treasure left in one place doesn't stay a secret for long. So he split it in two and hid each half in a different spot and wrote them in the old script that travelled inside email attachments, an alphabet and character combinations of **64** symbols that looks like gibberish to anyone who doesn't know the trick. Then he tidied up after himself, left a single word as a signature, and walked away whistling.

The repo looks spotless now. But this lab keeps a record of everything that ever happened in it, and a treasure hunter who knows where to look can follow Arjun's footsteps from the very beginning.

When you've found both halves, you'll need a good **chef**. Arjun always said, "Any scramble can be un-scrambled, if you have the **cyber** tools."

## Your Mission

Find Arjun's treasure. Bring the flag to a mentor to claim your prize. It looks like `flag{...}`.
